Snapslotify Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

What the app does

Snapslotify reads screenshots and photos that you choose, extracts event details such as title, date and time, and lets you add them to your calendar.

Offline processing

All processing happens on your device. The app does not use the internet. Your images and the text extracted from them are not uploaded, shared or sold.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we do not receive your images, text or calendar data.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps counters for its taps saved estimate on your device only. You can remove them by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

Report a problem

If you choose Report a problem, the app opens your email app with a prefilled message to bodhiarcade@gmail.com that shows the app version and device model. You may choose to attach a screenshot or image to help explain the problem. Nothing is attached unless you choose to attach it, and nothing is sent unless you send the email. You can edit or delete anything in the email before sending.

An image you attach may contain personal information, so please check it before you send it. If you send us an email, we use what you send only to look into the problem you reported, and we do not share it with anyone else.

Calendar

The app adds an event to your calendar only after you tap Add.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
